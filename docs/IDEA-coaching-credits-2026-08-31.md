# Coaching credit packages for in-person coaching

Came out of the Chiara meeting while discussing ongoing coaching at the centre.

## The idea

Riders buy a **package of coaching credits** up front. Michi publishes dates and
locations (Garda, and Tarifa too — "I could also start that in Tarifa already"),
roughly **once to three times a month depending on when and where**. Riders sign
up against a published slot and spend a credit.

> "we discussed the idea of some ongoing coaching, and during that talk it came to
> me that we should use credits for that — a credit system where people can buy
> packages of coaching with me."

## Reuse, don't rebuild

> "we had some credits for video coaching developed already. We may use that same
> thing for the real coaching."

The credit machinery in this project already exists for video coaching. The
in-person version needs slots, locations and a calendar on top of it, not a new
ledger.

## Constraints Michi named himself

- **He has to actually be there.** Publishing dates means blocking them, which
  costs location flexibility. "It'd be a bit less flexible where I am, when I am,
  so to plan in advance."
- **He needs a planning system.** "I should have a system of organizing things and
  planning things with a calendar for myself."
- **The split has to work for the centre.** Chiara has to earn from it too, or it
  competes with her instead of feeding her.
