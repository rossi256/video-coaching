Where the funnel leaks

You asked me to look at the whole registration process. Six things worth your attention, most consequential first. Nothing here is changed yet.

  • Biggest
    You cannot tell which channel works 44 of 45 signups are recorded as source: web. Exactly one says instagram. Last month you ran Instagram, stories, a WhatsApp broadcast and a newsletter, and the data cannot say which of them produced the 34 signups. Run the same push again and you learn the same nothing. Cheap fix, and it makes this campaign measurable: put ?src=email, ?src=wa, ?src=ig on each channel's link and pass it into the source column that already exists. About an hour, and it has to land before Wednesday to be worth anything.
  • Biggest
    The date is not above the fold On a phone the hero says "Live Q&A with Michi Rossmeier" and a generic line about meetups, with a button that only scrolls down. The next session's date and the actual signup button sit at 1257px on an 844px screen, so a cold visitor scrolls a screen and a half before learning when it is. Put the next session's date, time and a real Save my spot button in the hero. Your paid and organic traffic both land here.
  • Worth it
    Only the email deep-links Adding ?signup=next opens the signup immediately instead of dropping people on the page. The new email does this. The Instagram funnel DM still sends people to the bare page, which is the slow path described above. Change the link in the three comment-keyword rules in the funnels admin. Five minutes.
  • Worth it
    Only a quarter leave a question 11 of 45 signups wrote one, 28 of 45 picked interests. The submitted questions are what the companion builds your prep list from and what makes the hour feel made for the room. Three quarters arrive with nothing attached. The field currently reads "Any questions for the session? (optional)". Ask for one thing directly: "What is in your way on the water right now?" Same field, better prompt.
  • Worth it
    You still cannot count who attended Last month's real number is unknown. 9 people appear by name in the transcript and chat, against 34 registrations, and your own after-analysis says "plus silent attendees". The Zoom key has no reporting scope, so there is no attendee list. The same Zoom app update fixes two things at once: report:read:list_meeting_participants:admin gives you attendance, and cloud_recording:read makes the replay pipeline fully automatic instead of the one manual block you still do after every call.
  • Housekeeping
    No unsubscribe link, no copy to you, duplicate people The invite says "reply no more and I will take you off", which needs a human reading it. The Q&A emails do not BCC you, against your own rule for automated customer mail, so you never see what people actually received. And the list now holds Techno Mike twice under two addresses, so he gets two invites next month. Fine at 45 people. None of it is fine at a few hundred, which is where this ends up if the campaign works.
